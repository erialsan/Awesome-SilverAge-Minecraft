# GT New Horizons Wiki

The [GT New Horizons Wiki](<https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Main_Page>) provides community documentation for the 1.7.10 GTNH modpack, including installation and migration, server setup, mod usage, and troubleshooting.

## Reading Guidance

GTNH-specific recipes, configurations, and progression do not necessarily apply to other 1.7.10 packs. Read pages in their modpack context.

The Cleanroom machine or structure discussed within GTNH is unrelated to the [Cleanroom loader for 1.12.2](<../mods/1.12.2/cleanroom.md>).
