# 渡海工場事務所

[渡海工場事務所](<https://madoha-4862.hatenablog.jp/>) is a **Japanese-language personal blog** about Minecraft rendering, optimization, and shaders. Its [about page](<https://madoha-4862.hatenablog.jp/about>) provides profile information and links to articles.

At the time of review, the linked article titles covered Angelica settings, performance measurements and rendering issues on 1.7.10, GTNH shaders, and an in-development Iris port for 1.12.2.

## Reading Guidance

Use this as supplementary explanation and practical examples, not as an official specification. Check each article's date, mod versions, hardware, settings, and measurement conditions. The measurements have not been reproduced, and not every article has been reviewed for this list.
