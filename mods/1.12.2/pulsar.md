## Installation notes

At the time of review, the README requires Cleanroom 0.7.14-alpha or later within the 0.7.x line. It lists other lighting engines (Alfheim, Phosphor, Hesperus, Starlight-1.12), OptiFine, and Actinium 0.6.x as incompatible.
